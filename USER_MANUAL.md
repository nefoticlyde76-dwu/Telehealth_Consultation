## 1. Introduction

### What the system is

MBPHA TeleHealth is the official online consultation system of the Milne Bay Provincial Health Authority. It lets patients request an appointment with an MBPHA doctor, attend an approved video consultation, and later view their consultation record and prescription.

The system does not replace in-person care. It is a controlled way to offer remote consultations when travel to a health facility is difficult.

### What the system is used for

You can use MBPHA TeleHealth to:

- Create a patient account and sign in securely
- View doctors and the times they have opened for booking
- Request a consultation and track whether it is approved
- Cancel or reschedule your own booking when it is still early enough
- Join an approved video consultation at the scheduled time
- View and download consultation records and prescriptions after the visit
- Ask **MediMate AI** for general health information (patients only)
- Allow doctors to publish their available times, see colleagues’ hours on a shared calendar, conduct consultations, write notes, and issue prescriptions
- Allow administrators to review booking requests and manage user accounts

### Who can use it

There are three types of users:

| Role | Who this is | How they get access |
|------|-------------|---------------------|
| **Patient** | A person who needs an online consultation | Registers on the website, or continues with Google if that option is shown |
| **Doctor** | An MBPHA clinician | An administrator creates the account and sends an invitation email |
| **Administrator** | MBPHA staff who review bookings and manage accounts | An MBPHA administrator account is created by the organisation |

Doctors and administrators cannot register themselves on the public Register page.

### Main purpose

The purpose of the system is simple:

1. A doctor publishes available consultation times.
2. A patient books one of those times and explains the reason for the visit.
3. An administrator reviews the request and approves, rejects, or cancels it.
4. If it is approved, the patient and doctor join a video consultation at the scheduled time.
5. The doctor writes the consultation record, completes the visit, and may issue a prescription.
6. The patient can then view and download those documents.

## 2. Getting Started

### How to access the system

Open a web browser (for example Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari) and go to the official website:

**[https://mbphatelehealth.com](https://mbphatelehealth.com)**

If you are using a training or office copy of the system, your administrator will give you a different address. Use the address they provide.

On the public website you will see:

- **Home** — an introduction to the service
- **About** — who the service is for
- **How It Works** — the steps from registration to consultation records
- **Contact** — a form for general questions about the platform (this is not a booking form)
- **Login** and **Register** buttons

Use a computer or a modern phone or tablet with a camera and microphone if you will join a video consultation.

### Patient registration

Patients create their own accounts.

1. Open the website.
2. Select **Register**.
3. Complete **Patient registration**.
4. Enter:
   - **Full Name** (required)
   - **Email Address** (required)
   - **Password** (required)
   - **Confirm Password** (required)
5. Tick the box: *I agree to the use of my information for MBPHA telehealth services.*
6. Optionally open **Optional details** and add date of birth, gender, and address.
7. Select **Create patient account**.

If registration succeeds, you are signed in automatically and taken to the **Patient Dashboard**. There is no extra email-activation step for patients.

**Continue with Google** may appear if MBPHA has enabled it. This option is for patients only. Doctors and administrators must sign in with email and password.

### Login process

1. Select **Login**.
2. On the **Sign in** page, enter your **Email Address** and **Password**.
3. Select **Sign in**.

You are taken to the dashboard that matches your role:

- Patients go to the **Patient Dashboard**
- Doctors go to the **Doctor Dashboard**
- Administrators go to the **Administrator Dashboard**

Only an **Active** account can sign in. If your account is still waiting for a password setup, is suspended, or is deactivated, sign-in will fail.

### Doctor account activation

Doctors do not register on the public website.

1. An administrator creates the doctor account.
2. The doctor receives an email titled **MBPHA TeleHealth – Set Up Your Doctor Account**.
3. The doctor selects **Set Up My Password**.
4. On **Set Up Your MBPHA TeleHealth Password**, the doctor enters a new password and confirms it.
5. The doctor selects **Set Password**.
6. After the password is saved, the doctor signs in on the **Sign in** page.

If the invitation link has expired, ask an administrator to use **Resend Invitation**.

### Password requirements

Every new or changed password must be:

- at least **8 characters** long
- include an **uppercase** letter
- include a **lowercase** letter
- include a **number**
- include a **symbol** (for example `!`, `@`, or `#`)

The sign-in and registration screens show this as: *8+ characters with uppercase, lowercase, number, and symbol.*

### If you forget your password

1. On the **Sign in** page, select **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your **Email Address**.
3. Select **Send reset instructions**.
4. The screen then says that if an account exists for that email, reset instructions will be sent shortly.
5. Open the email titled **MBPHA TeleHealth – Reset Your Password**.
6. Select **Reset My Password**.
7. Enter a new password and confirm it.
8. Select **Save new password**.
9. Sign in with your email and the new password.

Reset links expire after a short time. If the link no longer works, request a new one from the sign-in page.

Password reset is not available for Google-only patient accounts that never set a local password. Those patients should continue with Google, or contact MBPHA administration for help.

## 3. Understanding the Interface

After you sign in, the workspace looks similar for every role. The menu items change according to your role.

### Sidebar (left menu)

The sidebar is the main menu. On a phone, open it with the menu button at the top.

**Patient menu**

| Group | Menu item | What it opens |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| Overview | **Dashboard** | Your home page |
| Care | **Book Consultation** | Weekly schedule of available times |
| Care | **Doctors** | Doctor directory |
| Records | **My Consultations** | Your bookings and records |
| Updates | **Notifications** | Your notices |
| Account | **Profile**, **Security**, **Preferences** | Your account settings |

**Doctor menu**

| Group | Menu item | What it opens |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| Overview | **Dashboard** | Your home page |
| Schedule | **Availability** | Shared calendar of all doctors’ hours, plus your own slot list |
| Schedule | **Consultations** | Assigned consultations |
| Updates | **Notifications** | Your notices |
| Account | **Profile**, **Security**, **Preferences** | Your account settings |

**Administrator menu**

| Group | Menu item | What it opens |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| Overview | **Dashboard** | Your home page |
| Operations | **Consultation Requests** | Booking review workspace |
| Management | **Users**, **Doctors**, **Patients** | Account management |
| Records | **Audit Logs** | Activity history |
| Updates | **Notifications** | Your notices |
| Account | **Profile**, **Security**, **Preferences** | Your account settings |

### Top bar

Across the top you will usually see:

- The MBPHA TeleHealth brand
- A shortcut button:
  - Patients: **Book Consultation**
  - Doctors: **Create Slot**
  - Administrators: **Review Requests**
- A **bell** for notifications (a number appears when you have unread notices)
- Your name or photo, with links to **Profile**, **Security**, **Preferences**, and **Logout**

### Dashboard

The dashboard is the first page after sign-in. It summarises what needs attention:

- Patients see upcoming and pending consultations, recent records, next available times, and notifications. A **MediMate AI** button is also available on most patient pages.
- Doctors see today’s schedule, upcoming consultations, recent completed visits, and availability coverage
- Administrators see pending requests, recent users, recent activity, and notifications

Summary cards are clickable. Use them as shortcuts.

### Profile and account area

**Profile** shows your identity details and photo.

**Security** is where you change your password and review signed-in devices.

**Preferences** is where you choose which optional notices you want to receive.

### Notifications

The bell opens recent notices. **View All** or the **Notifications** menu opens the full inbox.

From the inbox you can:

- **View** a notice (this usually opens the related consultation)
- **Mark read** or **Mark unread**
- **Delete** one notice
- **Mark all as read**
- **Delete selected**
- **Clear all**
- Open **Preferences**

Deleting a notice does not change any consultation record.

### Common screen elements

- **Cards** — white panels that group related information
- **Tables** — lists of doctors, slots, consultations, or users
- **Status badges** — coloured labels such as Pending, Approved, or Completed
- **Filters** — search boxes and dropdowns used to narrow a list
- **Buttons** — actions such as **Book**, **Join Consultation**, **Approve Request**, or **Save draft**
- **Breadcrumbs** — the trail at the top of a page (for example Dashboard → Slots → Book Consultation)
- **Empty states** — friendly messages when a list has no items yet

### Public website (before you sign in)

The public pages use a top navigation bar instead of a sidebar: **Home**, **About**, **How It Works**, and **Contact**, plus **Login** and **Register**. After you sign in, those buttons become **Dashboard** and **Logout**.

## 4. Patient User Guide

This section is for patients.

### 4.1 Patient Dashboard

After you sign in, you land on the **Patient Dashboard**.

The page subtitle is: *Check your next consultation, track request status, and book care when you need it.*

You will see:

- Summary cards: **Upcoming**, **Pending**, **Completed**, and **Unread Notifications**
- **Your Next Consultation**, with doctor, specialisation, status, date, time, reference number (for example `#TH-12`), and a next-step message
- **Recent Consultations**
- A short **Notifications** list
- **Next Available Slots**, each with a **Book** button

The main shortcut is **Book Consultation**.

On most patient pages (but not inside the video room), a **MediMate AI** button appears at the bottom of the screen. See [Section 4.9](#49-medimate-ai).

### 4.2 Finding a doctor

1. Open **Doctors** in the sidebar.
2. Review the **Find a Doctor** directory.
3. Each row shows the doctor’s name, photo, title, specialisation, **Available** status, and the next open times.
4. Select a time button to start a booking, or use **View All Slots** to see the full weekly schedule.

There is no search box on this page. If no doctors have published times, you will see *No doctors currently available*.

### 4.3 Booking a consultation

You cannot type any date and time you like. You must choose a time the doctor has already published.

**From the weekly schedule**

1. Open **Book Consultation**.
2. The page title is **Book a consultation**.
3. Use the filters if you wish: **Doctor**, **Specialization**, and **Week** (Previous / This week / Next).
4. Select **Apply**, or **Reset** to clear filters.
5. Open hours are highlighted. Each doctor has a colour so you can tell their times apart. A slot block shows the doctor’s name, specialisation, and time. If two doctors are open at the same hour, their blocks appear side by side.
6. The legend explains **Available — tap to book** and **Unavailable**.
7. Select **Book** on the time you want.

You can also switch to **Slot list** for a table view (the same doctor colours appear as small swatches), or start from the **Doctors** page.

**Confirm the booking**

1. Review **Doctor Information** and **Selected Consultation Slot**.
2. Enter **Brief Reason for Consultation (Chief Complaint)**. This is required and has a 500-character limit.
3. Optionally attach **Upload Complaint Image (Optional)** — a JPG, JPEG, or PNG photo up to 5 MB.
4. Select **Submit Booking Request**, or **Cancel** to go back.

If the booking is accepted, you will see *Consultation booking submitted successfully.* The request starts as **Pending**. An administrator must review it before you can join a video session.

If the time has already ended, is already reserved, or you already requested that same time, the system will ask you to choose another slot.

### 4.4 Viewing and tracking consultations

Open **My Consultations**.

When you are not using filters, consultations are grouped as:

- **Upcoming and active** — Pending and Approved
- **Completed consultations** — finished visits, newest first
- **Closed requests** — Rejected, Cancelled, and No-Show, kept for your records

You can also search by doctor or medication name, and filter by status, date, records, and sort order.

Open a row to see **Consultation Details** (or **Consultation Record** when the visit is completed). The detail page shows status, date and time, doctor, **Reason for visit**, and the complaint image if you uploaded one.

**Cancel or reschedule your booking**

If the booking is still **Pending** or **Approved**, and the appointment is still at least **24 hours away**, you will see **Reschedule** and **Cancel** on **My Consultations** and on the detail page.

**To reschedule**

1. Select **Reschedule**.
2. On **Reschedule Consultation**, review the current appointment.
3. Choose another **Available** time with the **same doctor**. You cannot move the booking to a different doctor from this page.
4. Confirm. The previous time is released for other patients.

A **Pending** request stays **Pending** and still needs administrator approval. An **Approved** request stays **Approved**; the video room is updated for the new time. The doctor is notified of the new appointment.

**To cancel**

1. Select **Cancel**.
2. Confirm in the dialog. The booked time is released.
3. Success message: *Your consultation booking has been cancelled.*

You cannot undo a cancellation from this page. Book a new slot later if you still need a consultation.

Cancel and reschedule are blocked when:

- The status is no longer Pending or Approved
- The appointment has already started
- There are fewer than 24 hours remaining (you will see: *Bookings cannot be cancelled or rescheduled within 24 hours of the appointment.*)
- There is no scheduled slot to move to (cancel may still be available)

If the buttons are hidden or a note explains why a change is blocked, contact MBPHA administration. An administrator can still cancel a pending or approved request.

### 4.5 Joining a video consultation

You can join only when all of the following are true:

- The request status is **Approved**
- The video room is ready
- The current time is within the join window: **10 minutes before the start time** until the **scheduled end time**
- Times are based on Papua New Guinea time

**Join Consultation** appears on **My Consultations** and on the consultation detail page.

Button labels you may see:

- **Join Consultation** — the room is open
- **Not Yet Open** — it is too early
- **Consultation Ended** — the scheduled time has passed

When the room is open, you will see *Your video consultation room is available now.* Your browser will ask for camera and microphone permission the first time you join.

If the consultation is still pending, you will see: *You can join once MBPHA administration approves the consultation.*

If the consultation was marked **No-Show** or **Cancelled**, you cannot join. Book a new time if you still need care.

### 4.6 Viewing records and prescriptions

After the doctor marks the consultation **Completed**, the detail page becomes a **Consultation Record**.

You may then see:

- Your original reason for visit and complaint image
- Your name, date of birth, gender, and address
- The clinical consultation record, once it is **Final**
- The prescription, if one was issued

When the documents exist, you can:

- **View Record**
- **Download Consultation Record**
- **View Prescription**
- **Download Prescription**

Downloads are PDF files. A record can be downloaded only when the consultation is completed and the clinical record is **Final**. A prescription can be downloaded only when one has been issued.

### 4.7 Updating your patient profile

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Review your photo, name, email, role, account status, date of birth, gender, phone, and address.
3. Select **Edit**.
4. Update date of birth, gender, phone, and address, then select **Save details**.
5. To change your photo, choose a JPG, JPEG, PNG, or WEBP file up to 5 MB and select **Save Profile Picture**.

Patients cannot change their full name or email address from this page.

Use **Security** to change your password. See [Section 9](#9-profile-and-account-management).

### 4.8 Patient notifications

Patients receive in-app notices such as:

- **Consultation Approved**
- **Consultation Request Rejected**
- **Upcoming Consultation** and **Appointment reminder** (when you are using the system near the appointment, including about 24 hours and 1 hour before)
- **Consultation Completed**
- **Prescription Available**
- **Consultation No-Show** — the scheduled visit was marked as not attended

Opening a notice usually takes you to that consultation. Booking approval is shown in the system as a notice. It is not sent as a separate booking-confirmation email.

### 4.9 MediMate AI

**MediMate AI** is a patient-only health information assistant. It is not a doctor and it does not replace a consultation.

A circular MediMate button appears on most patient pages after you sign in. It does **not** appear inside the video consultation room.

**How to use it**

1. Select the MediMate button.
2. Type a question, or choose a suggested topic such as *What is hypertension?*, *What is diabetes?*, *How can I prepare for my consultation?*, or *How do I book a consultation?*
3. Read the reply in the chat panel.
4. Use **New chat** to start a fresh conversation, or open previous conversations from the history list.

MediMate can help with general health topics and with how to use this TeleHealth system (browse doctors, book a slot, track a booking, and join an approved video consultation).

MediMate **cannot**:

- Diagnose your condition
- Prescribe medicine or tell you what dose to take
- Tell you to start, stop, or change prescribed medication
- Replace a booked consultation with an MBPHA doctor

If your symptoms may be serious or look like an emergency (for example severe chest pain, trouble breathing, or sudden weakness), seek emergency medical care. MediMate is not an emergency service.

If MediMate is temporarily unavailable, you will see a short message asking you to try again shortly. Close the panel and continue using the rest of the system as usual.

## 5. Doctor User Guide

This section is for MBPHA doctors.

### 5.1 Activating your account and signing in

See [Doctor account activation](#doctor-account-activation) in Section 2.

After your password is set, sign in with your email and password. You cannot use **Continue with Google**.

If your account still shows **Invitation pending**, you cannot sign in until you complete password setup.

### 5.2 Doctor Dashboard

The **Doctor Dashboard** subtitle is: *See today's workload, upcoming consultations, and what needs your attention.*

You will see:

- Summary cards: **Today's Consultations**, **Upcoming Consultations**, **Completed Consultations**, and **Unread Notifications**
- **Today's Schedule**
- Recent **Notifications**
- **Upcoming Consultations**
- **Recent Consultations**
- Availability coverage for the coming days

The shortcut **Manage Availability** opens the **Shared Availability Calendar**. **Create Slot** in the top bar opens a one-off time entry.

A readiness reminder asks you to keep your phone number, specialisation, profile photo, and signature up to date.

### 5.3 Managing your availability

Patients can book only the times you publish. The weekly view is a **Shared Availability Calendar**: you can see every approved doctor’s **Available** and **Booked** hours so you can avoid overlapping consultations.

**Shared weekly schedule (default)**

1. Open **Availability**. The page title is **Shared Availability Calendar**.
2. Use Previous week / **This week** / Next week to move between weeks.
3. Optionally filter by **Doctor** (including **You**) or **Specialization**. Select **Reset** to show everyone again.
4. Each doctor has a colour. A slot block shows the doctor’s name, specialisation, date and time, and whether it is **Available** or **Booked**. If two doctors have hours at the same time, the blocks appear side by side.
5. The legend explains **Available**, **Booked**, and **Open time — click to add yours**.
6. Click an empty time on the grid to add **your** hours, or use **Add Availability**.
7. Click **your** block to edit or delete it (only if it is still **Available**).
8. Click another doctor’s block to **view** it only. You cannot edit or delete a colleague’s hours.

Usual grid hours are **8:00 AM – 4:30 PM**, Papua New Guinea time.

**My Slot List**

Select **My Slot List** to review **only your** saved times in a table. You can search, filter by **Available** or **Booked**, and edit or delete only unbooked times. Use **Weekly Schedule** to return to the shared calendar.

**Create a single time**

1. Select **Create Availability** or **Create Slot**.
2. Enter **Consultation Date**, **Start Time**, **End Time**, and optional notes.
3. Select **Create Availability**.

New times are **Available** by default. Once a patient books a time, it becomes **Booked** and cannot be edited or deleted — even when it is your own slot.

### 5.4 Viewing assigned consultations

Open **Consultations** (**My Consultations**).

You will see assigned consultations grouped as:

- **Upcoming and active** — Approved and Pending
- **Completed consultations**
- **Closed consultations** — Rejected, Cancelled, and No-Show

You can search by patient name and filter by status and date.

Doctors do **not** approve, reject, or cancel bookings. That is an administrator task. Pending consultations are visible for your information only.

If a patient cancels or reschedules, you receive an in-app notice. After an approved consultation’s start time, you may mark it as **No-Show** if it was not attended. See [Section 5.12](#512-marking-a-consultation-as-no-show).

### 5.5 Joining and conducting a consultation

When a booking is **Approved** and the join window is open:

1. Select **Join Consultation**, or **Review & complete**.
2. The **Video Consultation** room opens in **Clinician view**.
3. Allow camera and microphone access when the browser asks.
4. Use the video controls during the call.
5. Select **Leave call** when you have finished the video part.

The room is available from 10 minutes before the start time until the scheduled end time. If you open it too early, the button shows **Not Yet Open**.

### 5.6 Recording consultation notes

While you are in the consultation room, use the **Clinical documentation** panel.

Required fields:

- **Chief complaint / presenting problem**
- **History / symptoms**
- **Clinical findings / assessment**
- **Diagnosis**
- **Treatment / medical advice**

Optional:

- **Additional clinical notes**

Select **Save draft** at any time. Drafts save automatically until you complete the consultation. The record status shows **Draft** until you finalise it.

You cannot edit notes from the room after the record is **Final**.

### 5.7 Completing a consultation

1. Review the notes.
2. Select **Complete Consultation**.
3. Confirm in the dialog *Complete this consultation?*
4. The system saves the latest notes, marks the record **Final**, and marks the consultation **Completed**.

Success message: *Consultation completed. The clinical record is now finalized.*

You are then taken to the prescription page.

All required clinical fields must be filled before you can complete the visit. Finalised notes cannot be casually edited afterwards.

### 5.8 Creating a prescription

On the prescription page you can add one or more medicines:

- **Medication name**
- **Strength / dosage** (for example `500 mg`)
- **Directions / instructions** (for example `1 tablet three times daily`)
- **Duration** (optional)
- **Quantity** (optional)

Use **Add another medication** if needed. Select **Save Prescription**, review the confirmation, and save again.

If no medicine is needed, select **Skip — no medication required**.

Before you can issue a prescription:

- The consultation must be completed and the clinical record must be **Final**
- Your **Digital Signature** must already be uploaded in **My Profile**

The signature is attached automatically. You cannot use another doctor’s signature. After a prescription is saved, it cannot be edited.

Patient and doctor details on the prescription come from the existing records and cannot be typed over on this page.

### 5.9 Viewing history and downloading documents

Completed visits remain in **Consultations**. Open **View Record** to read the historical record.

When documents exist, you can **Download Consultation Record** and **Download Prescription**. You can also view the patient’s complaint image if one was uploaded.

### 5.10 Managing your doctor profile

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Review specialisation, license number (if shown), professional title, employee ID, phone, photo, and digital signature.
3. Select **Edit**.
4. You may update **Phone Number**, **Specialization**, **Change Profile Picture**, and **Digital Signature** (PNG or JPG, up to 2 MB).
5. Select **Save Profile Updates**.

Doctors cannot change full name, email, gender, professional title, or employee ID. Those details are managed by administration.

Upload your signature before you need to issue a prescription.

### 5.11 Doctor notifications

Doctors receive in-app notices such as:

- **New Consultation** — a booking with you has been approved
- **Consultation Cancelled** — the patient cancelled the booking
- **Consultation Rescheduled** — the patient moved the booking to another of your times
- **Upcoming Consultation** and **Appointment reminder** — a consultation is due soon (including about 24 hours and 1 hour before, when you are using the system)
- **Consultation No-Show** — the visit was marked as not attended

Opening a notice takes you to that consultation.

### 5.12 Marking a consultation as No-Show

Use **Mark as No-Show** when an **Approved** consultation was not attended. This is different from completing a visit and different from cancelling a booking.

**When the button appears**

- The consultation is **Approved**
- The scheduled **start time** has already passed
- You are on **Consultations**, the consultation detail page, or (for administrators) the request review page

**How to mark it**

1. Select **Mark as No-Show**.
2. Confirm in the dialog *Mark this consultation as No-Show?*
3. The booked slot is released. The consultation moves to **Closed** with status **No-Show**.
4. The patient is notified in the system.

You cannot undo No-Show from this page. You cannot mark No-Show before the start time, and you cannot use it on a Pending, Completed, Rejected, or Cancelled request.

## 6. Administrator User Guide

This section is for MBPHA administrators.

Administrators manage accounts and review bookings. Administrators do **not** join video consultations, write clinical notes, issue prescriptions, or download consultation PDFs.

### 6.1 Administrator Dashboard

The **Administrator Dashboard** subtitle is: *Review pending requests, manage accounts, and monitor recent activity.*

You will see:

- Summary cards: **Pending Requests**, **Approved Consultations**, **Completed Consultations**, and **Unread Notifications**
- **Pending Consultation Requests**, each with a **Review** button
- Recent **Notifications**
- **Recent Audit Activity**
- **Latest Users**

The shortcut **Review Pending Requests** (or **Review Requests** in the top bar) opens the review workspace.

### 6.2 Reviewing consultation requests

1. Open **Consultation Requests**.
2. The workspace subtitle is: *Review each request, then approve or reject without leaving this workspace.*
3. The queue defaults to **Pending** requests.
4. Select a row to see the patient, doctor, specialisation, date and time, chief complaint, and complaint image.
5. For a pending request, choose:
   - **Approve & Next** or **Approve Request**
   - **Reject & Next** or **Reject Request**
6. For a pending or approved request, you may also **Cancel Request**.
7. For an **Approved** request whose start time has already passed, you may **Mark as No-Show** if the consultation was not attended. This is different from **Cancel Request**.
8. Use **Open full details** when you want the full-page view.

**What happens next**

- **Approve** — the consultation is scheduled. The patient and doctor are notified in the system. The video room is prepared for the appointment.
- **Reject** — the request is closed and that time becomes available again for other patients.
- **Cancel** — the consultation is no longer active and the time is released where appropriate.
- **No-Show** — the scheduled consultation is recorded as not attended. The time is released. The patient and doctor are notified.

Once a request is **Rejected**, **Cancelled**, **Completed**, or **No-Show**, no further approval actions are available.

You can filter the queue by search (patient, email, doctor, or request ID), status, doctor, and date.

### 6.3 Managing doctor accounts

1. Open **Doctors** (**Doctor Account Management**).
2. Select **Create Doctor** (or **Add doctor** from Users).
3. On **Create a new doctor account**, enter:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Phone
   - Gender
   - Professional Title
   - Specialization
   - Employee ID (optional)
4. Select **Create Doctor Account**.

You do not create the doctor’s password. The doctor receives an invitation email and sets their own password.

If the email cannot be sent, the account is still created. Use **Resend Invitation**.

On the doctor list you can:

- **Edit** the doctor’s details
- **Resend Invitation** while the status is **Invitation pending**
- **Reset Password** after the doctor has set a password
- **Deactivate** an active doctor
- **Activate** a deactivated or suspended doctor

You cannot reset a password while the invitation is still pending.

There is no separate “approve doctor” step. The account becomes **Active** when the doctor completes password setup.

Administrators cannot open or edit a doctor’s availability schedule. Doctors manage their own times under **Availability**.

### 6.4 Managing patient accounts

Patients register themselves. Administrators do not create patient accounts from this screen.

1. Open **Patients** (**Patient Account Management**).
2. Search by name, email, or address, and filter by status.
3. Select **Edit** to update full name, email, date of birth, gender, address, and account status.
4. Use **Deactivate** or **Activate** when access must be changed.

### 6.5 Managing all users

**Users** shows every account (administrators, doctors, and patients). Clinical notes and prescriptions are not shown here.

You can:

- Search by name or email
- Filter by role and status
- Open a user to view non-clinical details
- **Suspend** an account (temporary block)
- **Deactivate** an account (disabled, but kept for history)
- **Reactivate** an account
- **Reset password**, and optionally require the user to choose a new password at next login
- Permanently **Delete** a patient or doctor account when that action is allowed

The last active administrator account cannot be suspended or deactivated. Administrators cannot delete their own account from this screen.

Permanent deletion asks you to confirm carefully, including a confirmation phrase on the screen. Use this only when you are certain.

### 6.6 Activity and audit logs

Open **Audit Logs** (**Activity & Audit Logs**).

This is a chronological list of important security and workflow events, such as approvals, rejections, cancellations, reschedules, no-shows, and account changes.

You can search and filter by action, role, user, and date. Open an event to see more detail. This page is for review only. You do not edit consultation records here.

### 6.7 Administrator profile

Open **Profile** to update your full name, email, employee ID, profile picture, and password.

There is no separate system-settings page for mail, video, or site-wide options. Those are managed by the organisation outside this screen.

### 6.8 Administrator notifications

Administrators receive **New Consultation Request** notices when a patient submits a booking. Opening the notice takes you to the review workspace.

### 6.9 What administrators cannot do

- Join or conduct a video consultation
- Write or edit consultation notes
- Create or edit prescriptions
- Download consultation or prescription PDFs
- Publish or edit doctor availability times
- Register as a patient from an administrator account and then use administrator tools in that same session as a patient

## 7. Common Workflows

These are the most important end-to-end tasks, written as numbered steps.

### How to register as a patient

1. Open the MBPHA TeleHealth website.
2. Select **Register**.
3. Enter your full name, email, password, and confirm password.
4. Tick the agreement box.
5. Optionally add date of birth, gender, and address.
6. Select **Create patient account**.
7. Confirm that the **Patient Dashboard** opens.

### How to sign in

1. Select **Login**.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Select **Sign in**.
4. Confirm that your role dashboard opens.

### How to book a consultation

1. Sign in as a patient.
2. Open **Book Consultation**, or open **Doctors** and choose a time.
3. Use **Doctor** or **Specialization** filters if you want a specific clinician. Each colour on the weekly schedule belongs to one doctor.
4. Select an **Available** time.
5. Check the doctor and the date and time on **Confirm your selected consultation slot**.
6. Enter your brief reason for consultation.
7. Upload a complaint image if it will help the doctor (optional).
8. Select **Submit Booking Request**.
9. Confirm the success message and that the request appears under **My Consultations** with status **Pending**.

### How a patient cancels or reschedules a booking

1. Sign in as a patient.
2. Open **My Consultations** and open the **Pending** or **Approved** booking.
3. Confirm the appointment is still at least 24 hours away.
4. To move it: select **Reschedule**, choose another **Available** time with the same doctor, and confirm.
5. To stop it: select **Cancel** and confirm. The time is released.
6. If the buttons are not shown, the cutoff has passed or the status can no longer be changed. Contact MBPHA administration.

### How an administrator reviews a booking

1. Sign in as an administrator.
2. Open **Consultation Requests**, or select **Review Requests**.
3. Select the pending request.
4. Read the complaint and check the date, time, patient, and doctor.
5. Select **Approve Request** or **Reject Request**. Use **Cancel Request** only if the booking should be stopped. After the start time of an approved visit that was not attended, use **Mark as No-Show**.
6. Confirm that the status badge changes.

### How to join a video consultation

1. Sign in as the patient or the assigned doctor.
2. Open **My Consultations** or **Consultations**.
3. Wait until **Join Consultation** is enabled (from 10 minutes before the start time).
4. Select **Join Consultation**.
5. Allow camera and microphone access if the browser asks.
6. Stay in the room until the consultation is finished.
7. Use **Leave call** when you are done with the video.

### How a doctor completes a consultation and issues a prescription

1. Sign in as the doctor.
2. Join the approved consultation when it is open.
3. Write or update the clinical notes in **Clinical documentation**.
4. Select **Save draft** as you work.
5. Select **Complete Consultation** and confirm.
6. On the prescription page, enter medicines and select **Save Prescription**, or select **Skip — no medication required**.
7. Confirm that the consultation now shows as **Completed**.

### How a patient downloads records

1. Sign in as the patient.
2. Open **My Consultations**.
3. Open the completed consultation.
4. Select **Download Consultation Record** when the record is **Final**.
5. Select **Download Prescription** if a prescription was issued.

### How a doctor publishes availability

1. Sign in as the doctor.
2. Open **Availability** to see the **Shared Availability Calendar**.
3. Review colleagues’ coloured hours so you do not overlap their consultations.
4. Click an empty time on the grid, or select **Add Availability** / **Create Availability**.
5. Save the schedule.
6. Confirm your new times show as **Available**. You can manage only your own slots; **My Slot List** shows your times only.

### How a doctor marks a missed consultation as No-Show

1. Sign in as the doctor.
2. Open **Consultations** after the scheduled start time.
3. Open the **Approved** consultation that was not attended.
4. Select **Mark as No-Show** and confirm.
5. Confirm the status changes to **No-Show** and the slot is released.

### How a patient uses MediMate AI

1. Sign in as a patient.
2. Select the **MediMate AI** button (it does not appear in the video room).
3. Type a general health or “how to use TeleHealth” question, or choose a suggested question.
4. Read the reply. Start a **New chat** or open previous conversations if needed.
5. Remember that MediMate cannot diagnose, prescribe, or replace a booked consultation.

### How a doctor activates an invited account

1. Open the invitation email **MBPHA TeleHealth – Set Up Your Doctor Account**.
2. Select **Set Up My Password**.
3. Enter and confirm a strong password.
4. Select **Set Password**.
5. Sign in with your email and new password.

### How to send a general contact inquiry

1. On the public website, open **Contact**.
2. Enter full name, email, subject, and message. Phone is optional.
3. Do not include sensitive medical details.
4. Select **Submit inquiry**.
5. Remember that this form is not a booking request.

## 8. Statuses and Notifications

### Consultation request statuses

|--------|---------------|-----------------|

| **Rejected** | Administration did not accept the booking. | The patient may book a different available time. |
| **Cancelled** | The booking was stopped by the patient or an administrator and is no longer active. | The patient may book a different available time if needed. |
| **No-Show** | The approved consultation was not attended. This is recorded separately from cancellation. | The patient may book a different available time if needed. |
| **Completed** | The doctor has finished the consultation and finalised the record. | Patients can view and download documents when they are available. |

### Clinical record statuses

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Draft** | The doctor is still writing notes. Patients cannot download the record yet. |
| **Final** | The record is finished. Download is available after the consultation is completed. |

| Label | What it means |

### Account statuses

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|

| **Active** | The account can sign in. |
| **Suspended** | The account is temporarily blocked and cannot sign in. |
| **Deactivated** | The account is disabled. History is kept. |

### Video join button labels

## 10. Troubleshooting

### Cannot log in

**What may be happening:** The email or password is wrong, or the account is not **Active**.

**Check:**

- Caps Lock and the exact email address
- That a doctor has finished password setup
- That the account has not been suspended or deactivated

**What to do:** Use **Forgot password?** if you have a local password. Doctors with an expired invitation should ask administration to resend the invitation. If the account is suspended or deactivated, contact MBPHA TeleHealth administration.

### Forgot password

**What may be happening:** You need a new password, or you are using a Google-only patient account.

**What to do:** Use **Forgot password?** and follow the email. If no email arrives, wait a few minutes, check junk or spam, then try again. Google-only patients should use **Continue with Google**.

### Invitation email not received

**What may be happening:** The invitation was not sent, went to junk mail, or the link expired.

**What to do:** Check junk and spam folders. Ask an administrator to use **Resend Invitation**. Open the newest link only.

### Account still pending

**Patients:** Registration makes the account **Active** immediately. If you cannot sign in, the problem is usually the password or a later status change, not a pending registration.

**Doctors:** **Invitation pending** means you have not set your password yet. Use the invitation email. You cannot sign in until that step is done.

### Unable to book a consultation

**What may be happening:** The time is no longer available, has ended, or you already requested that same slot.

**What to do:** Return to **Book Consultation**, choose another **Available** time, and submit again. Make sure the reason for consultation is filled in.

### Cannot cancel or reschedule

**What may be happening:** There are fewer than 24 hours until the appointment, the visit has already started, or the status is no longer Pending or Approved.

**What to do:** Read the note next to the booking. If **Reschedule** or **Cancel** is hidden, contact MBPHA administration. Reschedule can only move you to another **Available** time with the **same doctor**.

### No available appointment times

**What may be happening:** No doctor has published open times for the week you are viewing.

**What to do:** Try **Next** week, clear filters with **Reset**, or check **Doctors**. If the directory is empty, wait until doctors publish availability, or contact MBPHA.

### Unable to upload an image

**What may be happening:** The file is the wrong type, too large, or the image is too big in pixels.

**What to do:** Use JPG, JPEG, or PNG, keep the file at or under 5 MB, and try again. Booking can still be submitted without an image.

### Consultation not appearing

**What may be happening:** Filters are hiding it, or you are signed in with a different account.

**What to do:** Open **My Consultations** (or **Consultations** for doctors), set status to All, and clear date filters. Confirm you used the same email you used to book.

### Cannot join the video consultation

**What may be happening:** The request is not **Approved**, it is too early or too late, the room is not ready, or camera permission was denied.

**What to do:**

- Confirm the status is **Approved**
- Join from 10 minutes before the start time
- Allow camera and microphone when asked
- Try again shortly if the room is not ready
- Use a supported browser and a stable internet connection

Administrators cannot join the video room.

### Notification not appearing

**What may be happening:** The notice was deleted, marked read, or turned off in Preferences.

**What to do:** Open **Notifications**, check All and Read, and review **Preferences**. Approval and completion are also visible on **My Consultations**.

### Session expired or you were signed out

**What may be happening:** You were idle for too long, another device was used to sign other sessions out, or the account is no longer active.

**What to do:** Sign in again. If you see *This account is no longer active*, contact MBPHA TeleHealth administration.

### Password reset link invalid or expired

**What to do:** Return to **Forgot password?** and request a new link. Use the newest email.

### Cannot mark a consultation as No-Show

**What may be happening:** The consultation is not **Approved**, or the scheduled start time has not been reached yet.

**What to do:** Wait until the start time has passed. Use **Complete Consultation** if the visit did take place. Use **Cancel Request** (administrators only) if the booking should be stopped before it starts.

### MediMate AI is unavailable

**What may be happening:** The assistant is temporarily offline, or you are inside the video consultation room (MediMate is hidden there).

**What to do:** Leave the video room if you need MediMate, then try the button again shortly. Book a consultation if you need clinical advice. MediMate cannot diagnose or prescribe.

### Doctor cannot issue a prescription

**What may be happening:** The consultation is not completed, the record is not **Final**, or no signature is on file.

**What to do:** Complete the consultation first. Open **Profile**, upload your digital signature, then return to the prescription page.

### Administrator cannot reset a doctor password

**What may be happening:** The doctor is still **Invitation pending**.

**What to do:** Use **Resend Invitation** instead of **Reset Password**.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

**Who can register on the website?**  
Only patients. Doctors and administrators are created by MBPHA.

**Do I need to confirm my email after I register as a patient?**  
No. A successful registration signs you in and takes you to the Patient Dashboard.

**Can I use Google to sign in?**  
Patients may see **Continue with Google** if MBPHA has enabled it. Doctors and administrators always use email and password.

**Can I choose any date and time?**  
No. You can book only times a doctor has published as **Available**.

**When can I join the video consultation?**  
When the booking is **Approved**, from 10 minutes before the start time until the scheduled end time.

**Can I cancel or reschedule my own booking?**  
Yes, from **My Consultations**, while the booking is **Pending** or **Approved** and at least 24 hours remain before the appointment. Reschedule stays with the same doctor. After the cutoff, ask MBPHA administration. An administrator can still cancel a pending or approved request.

**Why do I see other doctors’ hours on Availability?**  
The weekly view is a shared calendar. Every approved doctor can see colleagues’ **Available** and **Booked** times so they can avoid overlapping consultations. You can add, edit, or delete only your own slots.

**What does No-Show mean?**  
An approved consultation that was not attended. A doctor or administrator can mark it after the start time. It is not the same as **Cancelled** or **Completed**. The time is released so it can be used again if it has not already ended.

**What is MediMate AI?**  
A patient-only assistant for general health information and help using this TeleHealth system. It is not a doctor. It cannot diagnose, prescribe, or replace a consultation.

**Who approves my booking?**  
An administrator. Doctors do not approve or reject requests.

**Will I get an email when my booking is approved?**  
You receive an in-app notification. Check the bell and **My Consultations**. The system’s regular emails are password-reset and doctor-invitation messages.

**What if I miss the consultation time?**  
The join button becomes **Consultation Ended**. You cannot enter the room after the scheduled end. The doctor or an administrator may mark the visit as **No-Show**. Book a new time, or contact MBPHA, if you still need a consultation.

**Can I download my record immediately after the call?**  
Only after the doctor completes the consultation and the record is **Final**. A prescription download appears only if a prescription was issued.

**Can an administrator see my clinical notes?**  
Administrator account screens show non-clinical details. Consultation notes and prescriptions are not managed on those screens.

**Why does my doctor profile say the signature is “Not assigned”?**  
You have not uploaded a digital signature yet. Upload one under **Edit Profile** before issuing a prescription.

**Does the system send SMS?**  
No. **SMS notices (reserved)** is stored for possible future use only.

**Is the Contact page a way to book an appointment?**  
No. Use **Register** or **Login**, then **Book Consultation**. The Contact form is for general platform questions only and should not include sensitive medical details.

**What browser should I use?**  
Use an up-to-date browser. For video, allow camera and microphone access. Headphones are recommended.

**What time zone does the system use?**  
Consultation times are shown in Papua New Guinea time.

## 12. Important Notes

- MBPHA TeleHealth is for scheduled video consultations with MBPHA doctors. It is not an emergency service. If you have a medical emergency, seek urgent in-person care.
- The system does not replace clinic visits when a doctor or administrator tells you to attend in person.
- Keep your email address and phone number current so staff can identify your account and send invitation or password-reset messages.
- Do not share your password. Use **Log out of all other devices** if you think someone else used your account.
- Sign out when you finish, especially on a shared computer.
- Patients cannot change their name or email after registration. Ask administration if a correction is required.
- Bookings are requests until an administrator approves them. Do not treat **Pending** as a confirmed appointment.
- Patients may cancel or reschedule a Pending or Approved booking at least 24 hours before the appointment. After that cutoff, ask administration.
- Join a few minutes early. The room opens 10 minutes before the start time and closes at the scheduled end.
- **MediMate AI** gives general information only. It is not an emergency service and it cannot diagnose or prescribe.
- Doctors should check the shared availability calendar before publishing hours so they do not overlap a colleague’s consultations.
- Upload only images that help the doctor understand your complaint. Use JPG or PNG files of 5 MB or less.
- Doctors should upload a signature before they need to prescribe. A saved prescription cannot be edited.
- Administrators must not use this system to conduct clinical consultations or to change clinical notes.
- Notifications inside the system are the main way patients and doctors learn about approvals, completions, and prescriptions.
- The Contact form is not a booking channel and must not be used for sensitive medical information.
- This manual describes the system as it is implemented today. Features that are not in the live system — such as a patient messaging inbox, payments, laboratory results, or SMS alerts — are not available.
